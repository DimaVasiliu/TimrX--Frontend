Multi-Color 3D Printing — Paint Models in Up to 16 Colors

Turn AI-textured models into multi-color print files. Use Manual Paint 3MF or Meshy Auto 3MF, export full-color 3MF for Bambu Lab AMS, Prusa MMU and other multi-material printers, run Print Check, or order an FDM print from TimrX.

Multi-color print (MCP) on TimrX bridges the gap between AI-generated textures and physical multi-material printing. The AI textures your model with colors and patterns; the MCP tool quantizes those textures into 1–16 solid filament regions; the slicer-ready 3MF goes straight into Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer or PrusaSlicer.

Don’t own an AMS or MMU? The 3MF is slicer-ready, so any print shop with an AMS or MMU can run it as-is. Multi-color print-and-ship from TimrX is still in build — single-color FDM and resin orders ship from the workspace today.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is multi-color 3D printing (MCP) on TimrX?

MCP on TimrX takes a textured AI 3D model and converts it into a slicer-ready full-color 3MF file with 1–16 filament color regions. Compatible with any AMS or MMU printer including Bambu Lab X1 / P1S, Prusa XL and others. You export the 3MF yourself; multi-color print-and-ship from TimrX is still in build.

Which printers does the full-color 3MF work with?

Any multi-material printer that imports 3MF. Tested with Bambu Lab X1 Carbon, X1E, P1S and P1P with AMS or AMS Lite; Prusa XL with the 5-tool head; Prusa MK4 / MK4S with MMU3.

How many colors can one print have?

Up to 16 filament colors per print. The AI quantizes the model’s surface textures into the number of colors you pick. More colors look richer but extend print time and use more purge material — for most prints 4–8 colors is the sweet spot.

How much does multi-color cost?

Generating the 3MF is 5 credits per conversion — live today. The print-on-demand side (TimrX prints and ships you the multi-color model) launches summer 2026. Planned starting price for multi-color FDM is $12 per small print; final price depends on volume, number of colors and shipping. We aren’t the cheapest option; we focus on color accuracy and print quality.

Will the colors match the AI textures exactly?

Close, but not pixel-perfect. Filaments have a limited gamut compared to a digital texture, and the model is split into solid color regions rather than continuous gradients. The 3MF preview shows the exact regions before you commit.
