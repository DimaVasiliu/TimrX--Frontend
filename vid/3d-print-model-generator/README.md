# 3D Print Model Generator Assets

Upload future GLB models into this folder using the filenames below.

## Model Prompts

1. `dragon-miniature.glb`
   Prompt: Detailed dragon miniature in a flying pose with spread wings, perched on a round tabletop base, resin-printable fantasy figurine, strong silhouette, visible scales, supported wing membrane detail, watertight mesh, no ultra-thin unsupported parts.

2. `phone-stand.glb`
   Prompt: Minimalist adjustable phone stand with a wide flat base, rear support slot, cable routing channel, rounded edges, compatible with phones up to 7 inches, FDM-printable in PLA or PETG, 1.2 mm minimum wall thickness, no loose unsupported hinges.

3. `geometric-planter.glb`
   Prompt: Low-poly geometric planter with faceted exterior, circular inner cavity, drainage holes in the bottom, 10 cm diameter, modern desk object, FDM-printable, thick walls, flat stable base, watertight single-piece mesh.

4. `mechanical-gears.glb`
   Prompt: Set of three interlocking spur gears with 20, 30, and 40 teeth, centered axle holes, 2 mm module, printable tolerance clearance, flat faces, chamfered edges, FDM-printable mechanical demonstration model.

5. `art-bust.glb`
   Prompt: Abstract human bust sculpture with flowing organic shapes, smooth museum-style surface, shoulders and neck integrated into a stable pedestal, resin-printable, high-detail but watertight geometry, no fragile loose strands.

6. `replacement-knob.glb`
   Prompt: Replacement appliance control knob, 35 mm diameter, ergonomic ribbed outer grip, D-shaft cutout on the underside, flat front face with subtle pointer mark, heat-resistant functional design, FDM-printable, strong wall thickness.
