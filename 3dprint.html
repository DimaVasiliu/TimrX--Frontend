Before you print

Important considerations for AI-generated models.

AI models are not automatically production-ready. They are generated approximations. Always verify in a slicer (Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, Cura, OrcaSlicer) before committing to a print run.

Textures do not affect physical prints. For FDM/resin printing, only geometry matters. Focus on mesh quality, not visual appearance. Paint or finish the physical object after printing.

Use Meshy's Blender plugin for quick repairs. Install the Meshy for Blender plugin and use the one-click "Make Manifold" tool to auto-fix holes, remove internal faces, and make normals consistent.

Consider orientation and supports. Rotate the model in your slicer to minimize overhangs and support material. This affects print quality, time, and cost significantly.

How to generate a 3D model in TimrX

Every run follows the same friendly path: describe the object, preview the mesh, then post-process until it is print-ready. These quick guides mirror the cards inside the workspace so you always know what comes next.

Workflow Essentials

1. Craft a vivid prompt

Lead with silhouette, support with materials, finish with mood. The AI rewards concrete nouns and reference eras.

  • Mention size cues (figurine, tabletop, helmet).
  • Call out materials: "brushed titanium spine, matte ceramic plates".
  • Reference inspiration: "Ghibli + brutalist mix".

2. Preview & iterate

Use the low-latency preview to check proportions before you spend tokens on refinement.

  • Run a Print Check from the viewer toolbar — it analyzes wall thickness and highlights thin areas.
  • Pin successful prompts to reuse structure later.
  • Use Refine/Remesh buttons to add detail without starting over.

3. Publish & share

Everything funnels into My Assets where you can label versions, download GLB/GLTF, and generate cover art.

  • Export GLB for printers or GLTF for DCC apps.
  • Add tags (armor, character, prop) to keep history searchable.
  • Send assets straight to Community with one click.

Post-Processing

Why Remesh?

AI-generated models often have topology issues. Remeshing fixes these for successful prints.

  • Fixes non-manifold geometry
  • Closes holes and gaps
  • Creates uniform polygon distribution
  • Ensures watertight meshes

Presets & Controls

Start from a preset, then open Advanced Settings when you need custom density, resize, origin placement, or export-only conversion.

  • Print Ready: triangle mesh + STL-focused output
  • Figurine / Mini: higher detail for small resin prints
  • High Detail: maximum fidelity for large display pieces
  • Game / Low-Poly: real-time rendering only, not for printing
  • Advanced: poly count, resize height, origin, format-only

Export Formats

GLB stays in the job for preview, then add the extra formats your slicer or DCC app needs.

  • GLB: Workspace preview and universal handoff
  • STL: Classic print format
  • OBJ / FBX: DCC and game-pipeline friendly
  • USDZ / BLEND: AR previews and Blender delivery

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about the TimrX 3D Print Hub.

FAQ

What file formats are supported?

TimrX exports models in GLB and GLTF formats, which are compatible with all major 3D printing slicers including Cura, PrusaSlicer, Bambu Studio, and more. You can also import these into Blender, Maya, or other DCC applications.

How do I ensure my model is print-ready?

Always use the Remesh feature before exporting. This ensures watertight geometry with no holes or inverted normals. Run a Print Check to spot thin walls, and verify wall thickness is at least 0.8mm for FDM (2× your nozzle diameter).

Can I edit models after generation?

Yes! Use the Refine and Remesh tools to modify generated models. You can also export to Blender or other 3D software for detailed editing. The Texture tool lets you apply and customize materials.

What's the difference between Refine and Remesh?

Refine adds detail and improves the aesthetic quality of your model. Remesh optimizes the topology for 3D printing, ensuring clean, manifold geometry with proper edge flow.

How long does generation take?

Initial generation typically takes 2-5 minutes depending on complexity. Refinement and remeshing add 1-3 minutes each. Jobs run asynchronously, so you can queue multiple tasks.

Can I use my own reference images?

Absolutely! The Image mode lets you upload reference photos or sketches. The AI will analyze your image and generate a matching 3D model. Works great for translating 2D concept art into printable models.

Platform Overview

TimrX is a browser-based generation platform for 3D models, images and videos — all from your browser. No downloads, no installs. Create production-ready assets using text prompts or reference images, then export for 3D printing, game engines, or social media.

Available tools

  • Text to 3D — Generate 3D models from text descriptions.
  • Image to 3D — Convert reference images into 3D models.
  • AI Image — Generate 2D images via OpenAI, Google Imagen, Google Nano, FLUX.2 Pro, Ideogram V3, Recraft V4, or Nano Banana.
  • AI Video — Generate videos via Veo 3.1, Seedance 2.0 (Mini / Fast / Quality), Seedance 2.5, or Seedance 1.5 Pro.
  • Remesh — Optimize 3D model topology for printing or editing.
  • AI Texture — Generate PBR textures for any 3D model.
  • 3D Viewer — Real-time Three.js viewer with orbit, zoom, and pan.
  • File Converter — Free client-side 3D format converter.

Workspace layout

The workspace is split into three areas:

  • Left rail — Tool selector. Switch between Image, Model, Remesh, Texture, and Video panels.
  • Center — 3D viewer. Interact with your current model: orbit, zoom, pan. The toolbar below offers quick actions like Retry, Remesh, Texture, Download, and Share.
  • Right panel — Generation history. Browse, search, and filter all your past creations. Expand to a full gallery view.
Tip: Click any item in the history panel to load it into the 3D viewer instantly.

Remesh

Optimize a model's topology for 3D printing, animation cleanup, or export delivery. Select the Remesh panel from the left rail, choose the current model or upload a new one, pick a preset, then adjust size, origin, and export formats when needed.

Output Formats
GLB stays enabled for in-app preview. Add STL, OBJ, FBX, USDZ, or BLEND depending on your print, DCC, AR, or Blender workflow.

Credits System

TimrX uses a credits-based system. Every generation action costs a fixed number of credits. Credits can be obtained through one-time purchases or monthly subscriptions.

How credits work

  • Reserved on submit — When you start a generation, the required credits are reserved from your balance immediately.
  • Consumed on success — Once the generation completes successfully, the reserved credits are permanently deducted.
  • Refunded on failure — If a generation fails or is cancelled, the reserved credits are returned to your balance automatically.

Credit sources

  • One-time packs — Purchase a credit pack for immediate use. Credits never expire.
  • Subscriptions — Receive a monthly credit allocation. Credits refresh each billing cycle.
Note: Video credits are separate from general credits. Video packs add to your video credit balance, while general packs add to your general credit balance.

How subscriptions work

  • Monthly refill — Credits are granted once per billing cycle at the start of each period.
  • Yearly billing — Billed annually, but credits are distributed monthly over 12 months.
  • No rollover — Unused subscription credits do not carry over to the next billing period.
  • Wallet credits — One-time purchased credits are separate and never expire. They are used after subscription credits are depleted.
Tip: Yearly plans save up to 17% compared to paying monthly.

  • Failed generations — Credits are automatically refunded if a generation fails or times out.
  • Cancelled generations — Credits are returned if you cancel before completion.
  • No refund after success — Once a generation completes and delivers a result, credits are not refundable.

FAQ

What file format do generated models use?

All AI-generated 3D models are delivered as GLB files. You can convert them to OBJ, STL, or GLTF using the built-in File Converter.

Are my models print-ready?

Generated models are suitable for most FDM and resin printers. For best results, run the Remesh tool with Feature Preserving or Adaptive mode to optimize the topology before slicing.

What is the difference between Refine and Remesh?

Refine adds aesthetic detail and improves overall mesh quality (6 credits). Remesh optimizes the topology (polygon structure) for printing or further editing (6 credits). Use Refine for visual quality, Remesh for structural quality.

Can I upload my own models?

Yes. Use the "New" button in the 3D viewer to upload a GLB or GLTF file (max 50MB). You can also upload models directly into the Remesh and Texture panels for processing.

Is the File Converter free?

Yes. The converter is completely free, requires no account, and processes everything client-side — your files never leave your device.

Which video provider should I use?

Veo 3.1 offers the highest resolution (up to 4K) and style controls. Seedance 2.0 Mini is the most affordable and the fastest, so it suits rapid iteration. Seedance 2.0 Fast is the balanced default. Seedance 2.0 Quality offers higher quality at a premium and is the only tier with 1080p. Seedance 2.5 is a newer model with stronger motion and prompt adherence, at the highest per-second cost and a 720p ceiling. Seedance 1.5 Pro via fal.ai supports 12-second durations.

Do credits expire?

One-time purchased credits never expire. Subscription credits refresh each billing cycle — unused subscription credits do not roll over.

What happens if a generation fails?

Credits are automatically refunded to your balance if a generation fails, times out, or is cancelled before completion.

How long do generations take?

Most 3D model generations complete in about 1 minute. Remesh takes about 2 minutes. Video generation takes about 2 minutes. Times vary depending on server load.
