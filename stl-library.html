Frequently asked questions

What format are the files?

Every model is a standard .STL file inside a ZIP. They open in any slicer — Cura, PrusaSlicer, Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer and more — with no conversion needed.

Are the models actually print-ready?

Yes. Each pack is checked for watertight, manifold geometry so it slices cleanly. You may still want supports depending on orientation and your printer, but the meshes themselves are ready to go.

How do I get my files after paying?

As soon as payment is confirmed, your download link appears on the success page and in your TimrX workspace under your purchases. You can re-download it any time — access does not expire.

What printer do I need?

Any FDM or resin 3D printer works. Packs tagged for miniatures are scaled for 28–32mm tabletop use; larger display models suit FDM. The file size and model count are listed on every pack.

Can I use the models commercially?

Each purchase includes a personal and hobby printing licence. For commercial printing, reselling prints, or redistribution, contact admin@timrx.live about a commercial licence.

Does the All-Access Pass include future packs?

Yes. The All-Access Library Pass unlocks every pack listed today and every new pack we add later — at no extra cost.
