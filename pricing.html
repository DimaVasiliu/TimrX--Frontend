Pricing — Generation Credits and 3D Print Service

TimrX runs on a simple credit system. Each AI tool costs a set number of credits per use — no hidden fees, no confusing tiers. Buy credits when you need them or subscribe for a monthly allocation.

TimrX uses a credit-based pricing model that gives you full control over how you spend. Every AI operation on the platform — whether it is generating an image, creating a video, building a 3D model, or applying textures — costs a defined number of credits. This means you only pay for what you actually use, and you can allocate your budget across tools in whatever way suits your workflow.

Credits can be obtained through free signup credits, one-time credit packs, or monthly subscription plans. Subscription plans offer the best value per credit and include additional perks like priority processing and higher resolution outputs. One-time packs are ideal for occasional users who want flexibility without a recurring commitment.

The credit costs listed below reflect the computational resources each tool requires. More complex operations like video generation and 3D modeling consume more credits than simpler tasks like image generation. All prices are subject to change as new models and features are added to the platform.

Pricing FAQ

Common questions about how credits, subscriptions, and billing work on TimrX.

How does the credit system work?

TimrX uses a credit-based system where each AI operation costs a set number of credits. When you generate an image, create a video, or build a 3D model, credits are deducted from your balance. Different tools consume different amounts of credits depending on the computational resources required. More intensive tasks like video generation and 3D modeling cost more credits than image generation. You can view your remaining credit balance at any time from the dashboard, and the exact credit cost is displayed before you confirm each generation.

Do I get free credits to start?

Yes. Every new TimrX account receives free credits on signup — no credit card or payment information required. Your signup credits give you access to every tool on the platform so you can evaluate the full range of capabilities before deciding whether to buy more credits or subscribe to a plan. Once your signup credits are used up, you can either buy a one-time credit pack or subscribe to a monthly plan for ongoing access.

Can I buy credits without a subscription?

Absolutely. TimrX offers one-time credit packs in multiple sizes alongside its subscription plans. This pay-as-you-go option is perfect for users who have occasional or project-based needs and prefer not to commit to a recurring monthly payment. One-time credit packs never expire, so you can purchase them and use them at whatever pace suits your workflow. Subscription plans, however, offer better per-credit value and additional benefits like priority processing.

Do unused credits roll over?

Credits purchased through one-time packs never expire and roll over indefinitely — they remain in your account until you use them. For monthly subscription plans, rollover policies vary by tier. Some plans allow unused credits to carry over for one or two billing cycles, while others reset each month. The specific rollover terms are outlined on the plan selection page and in the terms of service. If credit rollover is important to your workflow, one-time packs offer the most flexibility.

Can I get a refund for unused credits?

Refund eligibility depends on the type of purchase and how recently it was made. Unused credits from one-time credit pack purchases may be eligible for a refund within a specified window after purchase. Monthly subscription fees for the current billing period are generally non-refundable once the credits have been allocated to your account. For detailed refund terms and to submit a request, please review the TimrX terms of service or contact support at admin@timrx.live.

How is the TimrX print-and-ship service priced?

Print orders are priced separately from AI credits. The cost depends on model volume, material (PLA / PETG / resin), infill, finish and shipping destination — full breakdown shown on the order screen before you pay. We aren’t trying to be the cheapest service; we focus on premium output, fair sustainable pricing and reliable delivery. Free worldwide shipping kicks in over $40.
