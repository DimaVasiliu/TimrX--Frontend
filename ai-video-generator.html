Prompt: "White sneakers rotating on a black pedestal, studio lighting, smooth 360-degree spin, clean background"

Freelancers and small studios use AI video generation to produce logo animations for clients without needing After Effects expertise. Upload your logo as an image and let the AI handle the animation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does TimrX AI video generator pricing work?

TimrX gives every new account starter credits to generate AI videos directly in your browser before choosing a paid plan. Your starter credits include standard-resolution output. After that, generation runs on credit-based plans — premium plans add higher resolution, longer clips, faster processing and expanded limits. No credit card is required to get started.

What video formats can I export?

TimrX supports exporting generated videos in MP4 and WebM formats. MP4 is the most widely compatible format and works natively on social media platforms, mobile devices, and video editors. WebM offers efficient compression and smaller file sizes, making it a strong choice for embedding video on websites. Both formats maintain high visual quality and consistent frame rates across all output resolutions.

Can I create videos from still images?

Absolutely. TimrX supports a full image-to-video workflow that lets you upload a still photograph, digital illustration, or AI-generated image and transform it into an animated video clip. The AI analyzes the composition of the image and applies realistic motion, including camera pans, zoom effects, and parallax movement. This is especially useful for e-commerce product shots, concept art presentations, and social media content where you want to turn a static image into something dynamic.

How long can generated videos be?

Currently, TimrX generates short video clips up to 15 seconds depending on the provider, generation mode, resolution, and parameters you select. Short-form clips are optimized for social media, motion graphics, and rapid creative prototyping. If you need longer content, you can generate multiple clips and stitch them together using any standard video editing tool.

How is TimrX different from Runway?

While both TimrX and Runway offer AI-powered video generation, TimrX stands apart through its integrated creative pipeline. Runway focuses primarily on video editing and generation features, whereas TimrX connects video generation to AI image creation, text-to-3D modeling, image-to-3D conversion, and even 3D print preparation. This means you can go from a text prompt to a video to a physical 3D-printed object entirely within one platform. TimrX also gives starter credits with no credit card required, making it more accessible for independent creators, students, and hobbyists who want to experiment before choosing a paid plan.

Can I use generated videos commercially?

Yes, all videos you generate using TimrX are yours to use for commercial purposes. This includes marketing campaigns, social media advertising, product videos, client deliverables, and any other business use. You retain full rights to the output content you create. We encourage reviewing our terms of service for detailed information, but standard commercial usage is permitted on every plan, including your initial signup credits.

What resolution are the generated videos?

With starter credits, videos generate at 720p resolution, which provides clear and sharp output suitable for most social media platforms and web applications. Premium plan users unlock access to 1080p and higher resolution outputs for professional-grade deliverables. All generated videos maintain smooth frame rates and visual consistency, ensuring that the quality meets the expectations of modern content platforms regardless of which resolution tier you use.

Can I convert AI videos into 3D animations?

Yes, and this is one of TimrX's most distinctive capabilities. You can extract individual frames from any generated video and use them as input for TimrX's image-to-3D or text-to-3D tools. The resulting 3D models can then be animated, textured, and exported for use in game engines, 3D visualization software, or prepared for 3D printing. This end-to-end pipeline from video to 3D asset is unique to TimrX and opens up creative workflows that are not possible on other AI video generation platforms.
