3D Print Model Generator — AI Powered Print-Ready Models

Generate watertight, print-ready 3D models from text or images. TimrX optimizes meshes for FDM and resin printers with built-in remeshing and GLB export.

3D printing starts with a good model. TimrX's AI generates print-optimized meshes from simple text descriptions or reference images, eliminating the need for manual modeling.

The built-in remeshing pipeline ensures watertight geometry suitable for both FDM and resin printers. Models are generated with uniform face distribution and clean topology.

From custom figurines and mechanical parts to artistic sculptures and functional prototypes, the 3D print model generator handles the entire workflow from concept to exportable GLB file.

How It Works

01

Describe or Upload

Enter a text description of the model you want to print, or upload a reference image for the AI to reconstruct.

02

Generate & Remesh

The AI creates a 3D mesh and applies remeshing to ensure watertight, print-ready geometry with clean topology.

03

Export for Printing

Download the GLB file and load it into your slicer. The model is ready for FDM or resin printing.
