01 / About

Engineering clarity into ambitious digital ideas.

I’m a full-stack developer based in London. I work across frontend interfaces, backend systems, database architecture and cloud delivery, with a particular focus on creative technology and 3D on the web.

I built TimrX from the ground up: a production platform for image, video and 3D generation with credit billing, job queues, authentication, asset storage and a Three.js workspace.

I approach every project with the same priorities: make the product understandable, make the interaction feel intentional, and build the underlying system so it can survive real use.
