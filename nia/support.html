Network Infrastructure Trainer

Network Infrastructure Trainer ("NIA") is a mobile training and self-assessment app for network infrastructure awareness, published as part of the TimrX portfolio. This page is the official support and contact point for the app.

Need help? The fastest way to reach us is by email. We aim to reply within 24–48 hours.

Common Requests

  • Refunds: Purchases are handled by the App Store / Google Play. See the Refund Policy for the quickest route.
