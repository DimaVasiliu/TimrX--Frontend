Network Infrastructure Trainer (NIA)

Network Infrastructure Trainer

Network Infrastructure Trainer ("NIA") is a mobile training and self-assessment app for network infrastructure awareness, published as part of the TimrX portfolio. This page is the official support and contact point for the app.

Need help? The fastest way to reach us is by email. We aim to reply within 24–48 hours.

Contact Support

For questions, bug reports, account help, billing, or refund queries, email us and we'll get back to you:

Email Support

We typically respond within 24–48 hours, Monday to Friday.

support@timrx.live

Common Requests

  • Something isn't working: Tell us your device model, OS version, and what happened — screenshots help.
  • Refunds: Purchases are handled by the App Store / Google Play. See the Refund Policy for the quickest route.
  • Privacy & your data: To access, correct, or delete your data, email us — see the Privacy Policy.
  • Feature requests: We welcome them — just drop us a line.
