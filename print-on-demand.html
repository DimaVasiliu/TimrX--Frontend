Order FDM 3D Prints Online

Generate or upload a 3D model on TimrX, run Print Check, get a server-side quote, pay at checkout and receive tracked delivery. FDM filament ordering is live now; resin can be quoted manually while automated resin checkout stays disabled.

TimrX prints, ships & delivers. You generate or upload the model in the browser — text-to-3D, image-to-3D, remesh, repair, print check and export — then order the physical FDM print from the same workspace. The checkout uses the same server-side pricing model as the backend order API.

No printer to own, no slicer to learn, no support material to clean. TimrX validates the completed GLB from your history, scales it to the current 256 × 256 × 256 mm build volume, estimates material and delivery tier, then creates a tracked print order after payment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the TimrX print service live?

Yes. FDM print ordering is live inside the TimrX workspace for completed printable models. Generate or upload a model, run Print Check, click Order Print, review the server quote and continue to checkout.

What does TimrX 3D print on demand cover now?

The live flow covers FDM filament prints from TimrX models, including material, color, size, infill, finish, quantity, packaging, delivery-speed selection, live quote, checkout, order confirmation and status lookup. Resin ordering is disabled for now and can be quoted manually.

Is TimrX the cheapest 3D printing service?

No, and we’re upfront about that. TimrX focuses on reliable custom production, quality checks, secure packaging and tracked delivery rather than racing to the bottom. The order screen shows the full price before payment.

How does the first-small-print offer work?

Eligible first orders can receive one small FDM print free. The offer is checked automatically against the signed-in account and shipping fingerprint during checkout; no code is needed.

What materials do you print in?

The live FDM catalog includes PLA, PLA+ tough, PETG, ABS, TPU flexible and PLA Silk, with a 17-color swatch catalog. Resin materials are present in the roadmap but disabled for automated ordering until resin fulfillment is active.

Where do you ship and how is delivery priced?

Checkout supports US, Canada, Great Britain, EU, Australia, Japan and an Other destination bucket. Standard, express and priority delivery tiers are priced from model size, packed weight and destination. Standard delivery becomes included above the market threshold shown in checkout.

Can I order an externally-made model, or only TimrX-generated ones?

The print order flow is built around models you generate or upload to TimrX so the print check and the order pipeline can validate the file. If you have an existing STL or GLB and want it printed by us, contact admin@timrx.live and we’ll quote it manually.
