3D Print on Demand — Create, Order, Ship & Deliver

Generate an AI 3D model on TimrX and we'll print, QC and deliver it to your door. FDM, resin and multi-color AMS supported. Premium output, fair pricing, no hidden fees.

TimrX prints, ships & delivers. You generate the model in the browser — text-to-3D, image-to-3D, multi-color, whatever you need — and we handle everything physical. Production on calibrated FDM and resin hardware, supports removed, model cleaned and QC photographed before despatch, then tracked worldwide shipping.

No printer to own, no slicer to learn, no support material to clean. The same print check that runs after every generation is the one we use to validate your file before printing, so you know in advance whether the model is production-ready.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does TimrX 3D print on demand cover?

Generating an AI 3D model, optionally remeshing / repairing it for print, then printing it on our FDM or resin hardware (with multi-color AMS / MMU available), QC and worldwide shipping. You don’t need to own a printer or use a slicer.

Is TimrX the cheapest 3D printing service?

No, and we’re upfront about that. TimrX focuses on premium output, fair sustainable pricing and reliable delivery rather than racing to the bottom. We don’t add hidden fees — the order screen shows the full price before you pay. Free shipping over £40 and a first-print-free offer on first orders make the entry cost low without compromising on quality.

How does the first-print-free offer work?

Your first paid order on TimrX includes one small print (up to 50 mm tall, single-color FDM) free of charge. You only cover shipping. Limit one per customer and applied automatically — no code needed.

What materials do you print in?

FDM: PLA (12+ colors), PETG, PLA-CF for functional parts. Resin: standard, tough and clear resins for detail / mechanical pieces. Multi-color FDM via Bambu Lab AMS up to 16 filament colors per print.

Where do you ship and how long does delivery take?

Worldwide tracked shipping. Typical delivery is 3–7 business days in the UK and EU, 7–14 business days for the US and rest of world after print is complete. Most prints complete within 1–3 business days of order. You’ll get a tracking link as soon as the parcel leaves the workshop.

Can I order an externally-made model, or only TimrX-generated ones?

The print order flow is built around models you generate or upload to TimrX so the print check and the order pipeline can validate the file. If you have an existing STL or GLB and want it printed by us, contact admin@timrx.live and we’ll quote it manually.
