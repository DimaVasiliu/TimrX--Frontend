AI Animation Generator — Rig and Animate 3D Models

Bring your 3D models to life with AI-powered animation. Upload a static model, choose a motion, and export a fully rigged and animated asset ready for games, film, or social media.

Animating 3D models has traditionally required deep expertise in rigging, keyframing, and motion curves. AI animation changes that by automating the most tedious parts of the process, letting creators go from a static mesh to a moving character in minutes instead of days.

TimrX uses AI to analyze your 3D model's geometry, generate a skeleton rig, and apply realistic motion data from a library of animations or from custom text descriptions. Whether you need a character walking, fighting, dancing, or performing a product spin, the platform handles the heavy lifting.

Every animated model can be previewed directly in the browser, fine-tuned with speed and blending controls, and exported in industry-standard formats that work with Unity, Unreal Engine, Blender, and other production tools.

Frequently Asked Questions

Below you will find answers to the most common questions about the TimrX AI animation generator. Whether you are a game developer looking to speed up asset creation or a content creator exploring animated 3D visuals, these answers should help you get started.

How does AI animation generator pricing work?

TimrX gives every new account starter credits to animate 3D models. Your starter credits include access to a library of standard animation presets such as walking, idle, and basic gestures. If you need advanced animations like combat sequences, longer clips, batch processing, or priority rendering speeds, paid plans are available with flexible monthly and yearly pricing options.

What 3D file formats are supported for upload?

TimrX supports GLB, GLTF, FBX, and OBJ formats for animation input. The platform automatically analyzes your model's mesh structure and applies an AI-generated rig before animating. For best results, upload models with clean topology and a T-pose or A-pose base position. Models with excessively high polygon counts may be automatically decimated to optimize processing speed without visibly affecting animation quality.

Does the AI handle rigging automatically?

Yes. TimrX's AI rigging system automatically detects the skeletal structure of humanoid and quadruped models and generates a complete rig with weighted bones. This eliminates the manual rigging step that traditionally takes hours in tools like Blender or Maya. You can review and adjust the auto-generated rig before applying animations, and the system supports custom bone configurations for non-standard character designs.

Can I use the animated models in game engines?

Absolutely. Animated models can be exported in FBX and GLB formats, which are directly compatible with Unity, Unreal Engine, Godot, and other major game engines. The exported files include the skeletal rig and animation data, so you can import them into your project and use them immediately without additional setup. Animation clips are exported as separate tracks, making it easy to wire them into state machines and animation controllers.

What types of animations are available?

TimrX provides a growing library of animation types including walking, running, jumping, idle breathing, combat attacks, dance moves, waving, sitting, and custom text-described motions. The AI can also blend multiple animation clips together for smooth transitions, and you can adjust speed, intensity, and looping behavior for each animation. New presets are added regularly based on community requests and emerging use cases in gaming, film, and social media content creation.
