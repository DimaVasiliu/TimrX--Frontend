1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide information to site owners, and enhance your browsing experience.

Cookies can be "persistent" (remaining on your device until deleted or expired) or "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser). They can be set by the website you're visiting ("first-party cookies") or by third parties ("third-party cookies").

Note: We also use similar technologies such as local storage and session storage, which function similarly to cookies but may store different types of data.

2. How We Use Cookies

TimrX uses cookies and similar technologies for several purposes:

  • Essential Operations: Enable core website functionality and security
  • Authentication: Remember your login status and account preferences
  • Preferences: Store your settings and customizations
  • Analytics: Understand how visitors interact with our website
  • Performance: Optimize site speed and user experience

3. Types of Cookies We Use

3.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable basic features like page navigation, secure areas access, and remembering your cookie consent preferences. The website cannot function properly without these cookies.

Can be disabled: No (required for basic functionality)

3.2 Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization. They remember your preferences such as language settings, theme preferences, and previously entered information. Without these cookies, some features may not work correctly.

Can be disabled: Yes (some features may not work)

3.3 Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. This helps us improve our site and services.

Can be disabled: Yes

3.4 Marketing Cookies

These cookies may be used to track visitors across websites to display relevant advertisements. We currently do not use marketing cookies, but this may change in the future with notice.

Can be disabled: Yes

6. Managing Your Cookies

You have several options for managing cookies:

6.1 Cookie Consent

When you first visit our website, you'll see a cookie consent banner that allows you to accept or customize your cookie preferences. You can change these preferences at any time.

6.2 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically:

  • View what cookies are stored on your device
  • Delete some or all cookies
  • Block all cookies or only third-party cookies
  • Set preferences for specific websites

6.3 Opt-Out Tools

  • Google Analytics: Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • General opt-out: Visit Your Online Choices (EU) or DAA Opt-Out (US)

7. Browser Settings

Here's how to manage cookies in popular browsers:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies

For detailed instructions, visit your browser's help documentation or All About Cookies.

8. Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you disable or delete cookies, you may experience the following:

  • Essential cookies disabled: The website may not function correctly. You may be unable to log in or use core features.
  • Functional cookies disabled: Your preferences won't be saved, and you may need to reconfigure settings on each visit.
  • Analytics cookies disabled: No impact on your experience, but it limits our ability to improve the site.

Important: Blocking essential cookies may prevent you from using the 3D Print Hub workspace and other key features.
