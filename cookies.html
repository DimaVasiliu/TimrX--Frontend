1. Overview

TimrX does not set any first-party cookies. However, this site uses browser local storage for basic preferences and loads third-party services that may collect data independently. This policy explains exactly what is stored and which external services are used.

In short: No cookies from us, no analytics, no tracking. We use local storage for your consent preference and load Google Fonts and YouTube embeds which are governed by their own privacy policies.

2. What We Store Locally

We use your browser's local storage (not cookies) to remember a small number of preferences. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server.

Key Purpose Storage Type
timrx_cookie_consent Remembers whether you accepted or declined the consent banner localStorage (persistent) Functional

No other first-party data is stored on your device. There are no session tokens, authentication cookies, or tracking identifiers.

3. Third-Party Services

The following external services are loaded on this site. Each may collect data according to their own privacy policies.

3.1 Fonts

All web fonts (Inter, Bebas Neue, Space Grotesk) are self-hosted on our servers. No requests are made to Google Fonts or any third-party font service. Your font data stays entirely within our infrastructure.

3.2 YouTube Embeds

The 3D Print Hub page embeds YouTube videos. When you load a page with an embedded video, YouTube (Google) may set cookies and collect data such as your IP address, viewing history, and device information. This applies even if you do not play the video.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · YouTube Privacy Controls

Note: Third-party cookies set by Google Fonts or YouTube are not controlled by TimrX. We cannot access, modify, or delete them.

4. What We Do Not Use

For full transparency, TimrX does not use any of the following:

  • First-party cookies of any kind
  • Google Analytics or any other analytics platform
  • Marketing or advertising trackers
  • Fingerprinting or cross-site tracking
  • Payment processing cookies (no e-commerce on this site)
  • Authentication tokens or session cookies

5. Managing Your Data

5.1 Consent Banner

When you first visit this site, a banner asks whether you accept the use of third-party services. Your choice is stored locally. To reset it, clear your browser's local storage for this site or use the instructions below.

5.2 Clearing Local Storage

To remove the consent preference stored by TimrX:

  • Open your browser's Developer Tools (F12 or Ctrl+Shift+I)
  • Go to the Application (Chrome/Edge) or Storage (Firefox) tab
  • Find Local Storage and select this site's domain
  • Delete the timrx_cookie_consent entry

5.3 Blocking Third-Party Requests

If you prefer that Google Fonts and YouTube do not receive your data, you can:

  • Use a browser extension such as uBlock Origin to block requests to fonts.googleapis.com and youtube.com
  • Disable third-party cookies in your browser settings
  • Use your browser's built-in tracking protection

Blocking Google Fonts may cause text to render in fallback system fonts. Blocking YouTube will prevent embedded videos from loading.

6. Browser Settings

To manage cookies and site data in popular browsers:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies

For detailed instructions, visit your browser's help documentation or All About Cookies.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy if we add new third-party services or change how data is stored. When we make changes:

  • The "Last updated" date at the top will be revised
  • Material changes will be communicated through a notice on our website
