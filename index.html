About TimrX

TimrX is the creative lab of Dima Vasiliu, focused on AI-powered 3D production and polished web experiences.

The flagship TimrX 3D Print Hub turns prompts or images into printable models with a fast, iterative workflow. Whether you're a game developer, 3D printing enthusiast, or digital artist, the Hub streamlines your workflow with intuitive tools.
