About TimrX Blog

TimrX Blog covers practical topics at the intersection of AI, 3D graphics, web performance, and digital product building. Articles focus on real workflows rather than abstract theory.

Expect step-by-step guides on Three.js viewer implementation, GSAP scroll animations, AI-assisted 3D generation, and lessons learned from shipping actual projects.

Who It's For Developers, creators, and founders who want to build better digital experiences.
What You'll Find Experience-based insights—not recycled documentation. Real experiments, honest results.
Recommendations Tools and services that have genuinely helped in real projects. Sponsored content is always disclosed.

Blog FAQ

What topics does TimrX Blog cover?

AI workflows, 3D web development (Three.js, WebGL), animation (GSAP), performance optimization, UX experiments, and monetisation strategies for digital products.

Is this blog beginner friendly?

Most articles assume basic JavaScript knowledge, but each post explains concepts as needed. Start with workflow guides if you're newer to 3D or animation.

Do you recommend tools or services?

Yes, when they've genuinely helped in real projects. Recommendations are based on hands-on experience, and sponsored content is always disclosed.

Can I use ideas from this blog in my own projects?

Absolutely. Code snippets and techniques shared here are meant to be adapted and built upon.

How often is new content published?

New articles appear when there's something genuinely useful to share—typically 2-4 times per month.
