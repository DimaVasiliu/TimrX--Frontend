What we're building

TimrX started as a simple idea: make 3D creation accessible to everyone. Here is where that idea is heading.

Our Vision

AI-powered 3D generation

Turn text prompts and reference images into printable 3D models. We are continuously improving generation quality and adding new capabilities like texture and refinement tools.

Browser-first workflow

No installs, no plugins. Generate, refine, convert, and preview models entirely in your browser. Your files stay on your device when using local tools like the converter.

Print-ready output

Every model is designed with 3D printing in mind. Export to STL, OBJ, or GLB and take your creation straight to the printer with minimal cleanup.
