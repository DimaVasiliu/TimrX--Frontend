Seedance Video Generator

Generate video with Seedance inside TimrX. Turn a text prompt or a still image into a short animated clip with natural motion, then download it.

Seedance is a video generation engine available in the TimrX workspace. It supports both text-to-video and image-to-video — start from a written prompt, or animate a still image into a moving clip.

Seedance is one of several video engines on TimrX, alongside Veo 3. Generating with more than one engine lets you compare motion and style and choose the best clip for your project.

Image-to-video is useful when you already have a frame you like — a generated image, a product photo, a piece of concept art — and want to bring it to life as short motion content.

01

Choose your input

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Seedance video generator?

It is the Seedance video engine available in the TimrX workspace. It generates short video clips from text prompts or from still images you upload.

Does Seedance support image-to-video?

Yes. Seedance can animate a still image into a moving clip, as well as generate video from a text prompt.

How do I generate a video with Seedance?

Open the video panel in the workspace, choose a text prompt or upload a source image, and run the generation. Download the finished clip as an MP4.

What is the difference between Seedance and Veo 3 on TimrX?

Both are video engines in the same workspace. They produce different motion and style; generating with both lets you compare and pick the clip that fits your project.

What format does the clip download as?

Seedance clips export as standard MP4 files compatible with every editor and social platform.

How long are Seedance clips?

Seedance produces short clips designed for social posts, motion content and previz. Combine several clips in an editor for longer sequences.

Does Seedance generation cost credits?

Video generation runs on credits. New accounts get starter credits to test it; credit plans cover ongoing use. See the pricing page for current plans.
