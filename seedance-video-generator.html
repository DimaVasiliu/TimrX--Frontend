Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Seedance video generator?

It is the Seedance video engine inside the TimrX workspace. It generates short MP4 video clips from text prompts, still images, frame pairs, or reference media depending on the selected mode.

Does Seedance support image-to-video?

Yes. Seedance can animate a still image into a moving clip, generate from text, create first-to-last-frame transitions and use reference-led generation modes.

How do I generate a video with Seedance?

Open the video panel in the workspace, choose a text prompt or upload a source image, and run the generation. Download the finished clip as an MP4.

What is the difference between Seedance and Veo 3.1 on TimrX?

Both are video engines in the same workspace, but they produce different motion, camera behavior and style. Running the same brief through both gives you more options before choosing a final take.

What format does the clip download as?

Seedance clips export as standard MP4 files compatible with every editor and social platform.

How long are Seedance clips?

Seedance 2.5 generates clips up to 30 seconds in a single shot in the TimrX workspace; the Seedance 2.0 tiers produce up to 15 seconds. Combine several clips in an editor for longer sequences.

Which Seedance tiers are available?

The backend supports Seedance 2.0 Mini, Fast and Quality tiers, plus Seedance 2.5. The right choice depends on draft speed, target resolution, duration and credit budget.

Does Seedance generation cost credits?

Video generation runs on credits. New accounts get starter credits to test it; credit plans cover ongoing use. See the pricing page for current plans.
