Imagen Image Generator

Generate images with Imagen inside the TimrX workspace. Turn text prompts into concept art, character designs, product visuals, source frames and references you can continue into video or image-to-3D.

Imagen is a text-to-image generation engine available in the TimrX workspace. Describe what you want in a prompt and Imagen produces an image you can preview, refine and download.

Imagen is one of several image engines on TimrX, alongside FLUX, Ideogram and others. Generating with more than one engine lets you compare style and quality and pick the best result for your project.

Generated images suit concept art, character and product design, textures and reference frames — including source images you can feed into image-to-3D and image-to-video generation in the same workspace.

How It Works

01

Write a prompt

Describe the image — subject, style, lighting and composition. Specific prompts give more controllable results.

02

Generate with Imagen

Imagen produces an image from your prompt. Review it and refine the prompt to iterate toward the result you want.

03

Download or reuse

Download the image, or use it as a reference frame for image-to-3D or image-to-video generation on TimrX.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Imagen image generator on TimrX?

It is the Imagen text-to-image engine made available inside the TimrX workspace. You write a prompt and Imagen generates an image you can preview, refine and download.

How do I generate an image with Imagen?

Open the image panel in the workspace, write a prompt describing the subject and style, and run the generation. The result can be downloaded or reused in other tools.

What prompts work best with Imagen?

Describe the subject, style, lighting and composition clearly. Specific, concrete prompts give you more control over the result than short vague ones.

Can I use Imagen images for image-to-3D?

Yes. A generated image can be used as the source for image-to-3D and image-to-video generation in the same TimrX workspace.

Are there other image engines on TimrX?

Yes. TimrX offers multiple image engines including Imagen, FLUX and Ideogram, so you can compare results and choose the best one for your project.

What format do generated images download as?

Images download as standard image files ready for editors, presentations and the web.

Does image generation cost credits?

Image generation runs on credits. New accounts get starter credits to test it; credit plans cover ongoing use. See the pricing page for current plans.
