AI Game Asset Generator — Create Game-Ready 3D Models

Generate production-quality 3D game assets from text prompts. Characters, vehicles, weapons, and environments — optimized for Unity, Unreal Engine, and Godot.

AI game asset generation is transforming how studios and solo developers build their games. Instead of spending weeks modeling, texturing, and optimizing individual assets by hand, you describe what you need and the AI delivers a game-ready 3D model in seconds.

TimrX brings this capability into a single browser-based workspace. The platform generates 3D assets with clean topology, proper UV unwrapping, and optimized polygon counts that import directly into your game engine without manual cleanup or re-targeting.

Whether you are building a massive open-world RPG or a minimalist mobile puzzle game, TimrX adapts to your art direction and technical requirements, producing assets that match your visual style and performance budget from the very first generation.

How It Works

01

Describe Your Asset

Enter a text prompt describing the game asset you need. Specify the type, art style, polygon budget, and any engine-specific requirements.

02

Generate & Optimize

The AI generates your 3D asset with textures, proper topology, and optimized polygon counts. Refine with additional prompts or adjust parameters until the asset meets your needs.

03

Export for Engine

Download your asset in GLB, FBX, or OBJ format and import it directly into Unity, Unreal Engine, Godot, or any other game engine.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about generating game assets with TimrX. Whether you are shipping your first indie title or managing assets for a large studio, these details will help you understand how TimrX fits into your game development pipeline.

What game engines are supported?

TimrX generates assets compatible with all major game engines including Unity, Unreal Engine, and Godot. Assets are exported in standard formats such as GLB, FBX, and OBJ with optimized polygon counts and properly configured UV maps, so they can be imported directly into your engine of choice without additional conversion steps. The platform also supports engine-specific material setups, meaning textures are exported with the correct PBR channels for each engine's rendering pipeline.

Are the generated assets optimized for real-time rendering?

Yes, all generated game assets are optimized for real-time performance. The AI produces models with efficient polygon counts, clean topology, and properly unwrapped UVs. You can specify target polygon budgets during generation to ensure assets meet your project's performance requirements, whether you are building for mobile, console, or PC. LOD (level-of-detail) variants can also be generated automatically, giving you multiple resolution versions of the same asset for distance-based rendering.

Can I generate characters with animations?

TimrX generates fully rigged character models that are animation-ready. The AI applies standard skeletal rigs compatible with popular animation libraries like Mixamo, so you can apply walk cycles, combat animations, and custom motion capture data immediately after export. Rigging is handled automatically based on the character type detected in your prompt, whether it is a humanoid, quadruped, or custom creature.

What types of game assets can I create?

You can generate a wide range of game assets including player characters, NPCs, vehicles, weapons, props, environmental objects, buildings, terrain features, and UI elements. The AI handles both stylized and realistic art directions, so whether your game uses low-poly aesthetics, cel-shaded visuals, or photorealistic rendering, TimrX can produce assets that match your project's visual identity consistently.

Can I use the generated assets in commercial games?

Assets generated on paid TimrX plans include a full commercial-use license, meaning you can ship them in commercial games distributed on Steam, consoles, mobile app stores, or any other platform. Free-tier assets are licensed for personal and non-commercial use, which covers prototyping, learning, and game jam entries. Review the terms of service for complete licensing details before publishing.
