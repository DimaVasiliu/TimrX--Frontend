Meshy-Powered 3D Generator

Generate 3D models with Meshy inside the TimrX workspace. Turn a text prompt or image into a textured mesh, then refine, remesh, texture, rig, animate, print-check and export it.

Meshy is a 3D generation engine available in the TimrX workspace. It turns a text prompt or a reference image into a textured 3D mesh, so you can go from an idea to a usable model without manual modelling.

Meshy is one of several engines on TimrX — you generate, then stay in the same workspace to remesh, refine, texture, rig, animate, run a print check and export. The engine creates the draft; the platform takes it to a finished asset.

Meshy-generated models export as STL for 3D printing, and GLB and OBJ for game engines, Blender and rendering — the same export pipeline as every other model on TimrX.

How It Works

01

Describe or upload

Write a text prompt describing the object, or upload a reference image for Meshy to reconstruct in 3D.

02

Generate with Meshy

Meshy builds a textured 3D mesh from your input. Preview it in the 3D viewer and iterate on the prompt.

03

Refine and export

Remesh, texture and print-check the model, then export STL, GLB or OBJ for printing, games or rendering.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Meshy 3D generator on TimrX?

It is the Meshy 3D generation engine made available inside the TimrX workspace. You provide a text prompt or image and Meshy generates a textured 3D mesh you can refine and export.

Does Meshy support text-to-3D and image-to-3D?

Yes. You can generate a model from a written description or from a reference image, both through the Meshy engine in the workspace.

What can I do with a Meshy-generated model on TimrX?

After generating, you can remesh, refine, texture, rig, animate and run a print check on the model, then export it — all in the same browser workspace.

What formats can I export Meshy models in?

Meshy models export as STL for 3D printing and GLB and OBJ for game engines, Blender and rendering.

Are there other 3D engines on TimrX?

Yes. TimrX offers more than one 3D generation engine, so you can generate with different models and compare results before refining and exporting.

Can I 3D print a Meshy model?

Yes. Remesh the model to make it watertight, run a print check, and export an STL ready for your slicer.
