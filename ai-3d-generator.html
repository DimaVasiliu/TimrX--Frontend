Frequently Asked Questions

How does TimrX AI 3D generator pricing work?

Every new TimrX account gets starter credits to generate 3D models from text or images directly in your browser. There is no software to install and no plugin to configure. Your starter credits cover text-to-3D generation, image-to-3D conversion, basic remeshing, and GLB export, and generation continues on credit-based plans. Full-color 3MF print conversion is available for 10 credits.

What 3D formats can I export?

TimrX supports GLB, OBJ, FBX, STL, USDZ, and 3MF export. GLB is ideal for web viewers and game engines. STL is the standard for single-color 3D printing. 3MF is the modern format for multi-color printers (Bambu Lab AMS, Prusa MMU) — it maps textures into slicer-ready color regions with 1-16 filament colors. USDZ enables AR Quick Look on Apple devices.

Can I use generated 3D models in Unity or Unreal?

Absolutely. Export your model as a GLB or GLTF file and drag it into your Unity or Unreal Engine project. The file includes mesh geometry, UV maps, and PBR-compatible textures. Materials transfer automatically in most cases, reducing manual setup time when populating game scenes with AI-generated assets.

How detailed are the generated 3D models?

Model detail depends on prompt specificity and the complexity of the subject. Simple objects like cups or tools produce clean, game-ready meshes with minimal cleanup. Complex subjects like characters or vehicles benefit from the built-in remeshing tools, which let you adjust polygon density, smooth surfaces, and optimize topology before export.

Can I 3D print the generated models?

Yes. Use TimrX's remeshing pipeline to ensure the mesh is manifold (watertight) before exporting as STL or GLB. Import the file into your preferred slicer, set your layer height and infill, and print. Many users have successfully printed figurines, mechanical parts, and architectural models generated entirely by the AI.

How is TimrX different from Meshy?

Both platforms offer text-to-3D and image-to-3D capabilities, but TimrX differentiates itself with built-in remeshing, texture editing, and multi-format export including USDZ. TimrX runs entirely in the browser with no account wall to start. Meshy requires registration and caps starter generations more tightly. TimrX also supports STL export natively, making it a better fit for 3D printing workflows.

Can I generate 3D models from photos?

Yes. Upload a reference photo and TimrX's image-to-3D pipeline interprets the visual content to generate a textured 3D mesh. This works best with clear, well-lit images of single objects against a neutral background. The AI extracts shape, color, and surface detail to produce a model you can further refine with the built-in editing tools.

Can I rig and animate the generated models?

Generated models export as standard GLB/GLTF files fully compatible with rigging and animation workflows. Import into Blender for manual rigging, or upload to Mixamo for automatic skeleton fitting and animation. TimrX's remeshing tools help produce clean quad-dominant topology that deforms smoothly during skeletal animation, reducing artifacts in joints and limbs.
