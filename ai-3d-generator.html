AI 3D Generator — Create 3D Models from Text or Images

Generate 3D meshes from text descriptions or reference images. TimrX handles geometry creation, texturing, remeshing, and GLB export in one pipeline.

AI 3D generation eliminates the need for manual modeling in many creative workflows. Describe an object or upload a reference image, and the AI produces a textured 3D mesh ready for rendering, animation, or 3D printing.

TimrX combines text-to-3D and image-to-3D capabilities with built-in remeshing, texture painting, and format conversion. The result is a complete 3D asset pipeline accessible through your browser.

Generated models export as GLB files compatible with Blender, Unity, Unreal Engine, and most 3D printing slicers. From concept to printable model in minutes.

How It Works

01

Input Text or Image

Type a description of the 3D object you want, or upload a reference image. The AI interprets both to generate geometry.

02

Generate & Refine

The AI creates a textured 3D mesh. Use remeshing tools to optimize geometry and adjust textures to your needs.

03

Export GLB

Download your model as a GLB file. Use it in 3D software, game engines, web viewers, or send it to a 3D printer.
