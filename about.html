The Project

What TimrX Is

TimrX is an AI-powered 3D generation platform I built from the ground up. The 3D Print Hub handles text-to-3D, image-to-3D, remeshing, retexturing, and exports to formats like GLB, STL, and USDZ.

It's a complete system: credit-based billing, job queuing, user authentication, a Three.js-powered viewer, and a modular Flask backend with PostgreSQL. The platform is in active development, with new capabilities shipping regularly.
