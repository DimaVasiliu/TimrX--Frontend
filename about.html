Who I Am

I'm a full-stack developer based in Europe, focused on building tools that bridge the gap between AI, 3D graphics, and practical workflows. My work combines technical depth with a focus on user experience and performance.

I've been writing code professionally for several years, with a particular interest in WebGL, Three.js, and the intersection of AI and creative tools.

What TimrX Is

TimrX is a platform I built to explore what's possible when you combine AI-powered 3D generation with practical, real-world workflows. The centerpiece is the 3D Print Hub, which lets you generate 3D models from text or images and export them for 3D printing or other uses.

It's not just a demo. It's a working tool that I use myself and continue to improve based on real feedback and use cases.

Why I Write About This

The TimrX Blog exists because I believe in sharing what actually works. Too much content online is recycled documentation or hype. I write about real experiments, including the failures and edge cases that don't make it into tutorials.

Topics I cover include Three.js implementation details, GSAP animation techniques, AI workflow optimization, and web performance. If I haven't actually built something with a technique, I don't write about it.
